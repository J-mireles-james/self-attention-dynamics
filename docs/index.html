PROJECTED SELF-ATTENTION · DYNAMICAL SYSTEMS

From Low-Dimensional Chaos to High-Dimensional Dynamics in Self-Attention Systems

J. D. Mireles James · Florida Atlantic University · November 2026

We study autonomous projected self-attention systems with constant query, key, and value matrices, without imposing the symmetry assumptions that yield gradient dynamics. The analysis includes explicit periodic solutions for three particles on an ellipse, numerical investigations of recurrent dynamics, and constructions of higher-dimensional systems through structured attention coupling.

Manuscript (PDF) ↗ MATLAB code and data ↗

The numerical results provide evidence for invariant-torus-like and chaotic dynamics; they do not constitute computer-assisted proofs of the existence of these invariant sets.

01 / Research problem

Beyond the gradient setting

Under appropriate symmetry assumptions on the query, key, and value matrices, projected self-attention has a gradient formulation. In the analytic setting considered here, the gradient structure implies convergence to equilibria and precludes nonconstant periodic orbits.

We relax these symmetry assumptions while keeping the attention matrices constant. For three particles constrained to an ellipse, the angular equations define a vector field on the three-dimensional torus, allowing for the investigation of non-gradient recurrent dynamics.

02 / Selected findings

Analytical results and numerical observations

The paper combines explicit calculations for synchronized solutions with numerical studies of periodic, torus-like, and chaotic behavior. It also examines coupled systems of up to sixty particles.

A · Analytical

An explicit periodic orbit

When the effective matrix has nonreal eigenvalues, the fully synchronized configuration defines a periodic orbit. Its period and two nontrivial Floquet multipliers are obtained explicitly (Proposition 4).

Periodic, torus-like, and chaotic regimes

Parameter scans, Poincaré sections, continuation of periodic orbits, and finite-time Lyapunov exponents provide evidence for attracting nonsynchronized periodic orbits, torus-like dynamics, and chaotic regimes.

C · Numerical & structural

Dependence on coupling architecture

Block-masked single-head attention and the group-label formulation define the same coupled vector field. Separately normalized two-head attention gives a different system, with distinct numerical Lyapunov spectra at the same nominal coupling parameter.

03 / Reproducibility

Numerical code and data

The repository contains MATLAB driver scripts for Figures 1–9 and Tables 1–2, along with supporting functions and archived numerical datasets.

Each driver loads the corresponding archived data by default. The option recompute = true reruns the associated numerical calculation. The parameter scans and higher-dimensional Lyapunov computations can require substantial computing time.
